# Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Policy

**Northwind Pay, Inc. — Synthetic sample document for demonstration purposes only.**
Policy owner: Chief Compliance Officer. Version 4.2. Effective 1 January 2026.

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes Northwind Pay's framework for preventing the use of its
products and services for money laundering, terrorist financing, and sanctions
evasion. It defines the controls every employee, contractor, and partner must
follow, and it aligns the firm's obligations with applicable regulations in the
jurisdictions where Northwind Pay operates.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all business units, all employees, and all customer
relationships, including individual consumers, sole traders, and corporate
entities. It governs the full customer lifecycle: onboarding, ongoing monitoring,
and offboarding. No product may be launched without an AML risk assessment
approved by the Compliance team.

## 3. Risk-Based Approach

Northwind Pay applies a risk-based approach. Controls are proportionate to the
money-laundering risk a customer or transaction presents. Risk is assessed across
four factors: customer type, geography, product, and channel. Higher-risk
relationships receive enhanced controls and more frequent review; lower-risk
relationships receive standard controls.

## 4. Customer Risk Rating

Every customer is assigned a risk rating of Low, Medium, or High at onboarding and
re-evaluated at least annually. High-risk customers must be reviewed every six
months. The following always trigger a High rating: politically exposed persons
(PEPs), customers in sanctioned or high-risk jurisdictions, and customers whose
source of funds cannot be reasonably established. A High rating requires sign-off
by a Compliance Officer before the account may transact.

## 5. Transaction Monitoring

All customer transactions are screened by automated monitoring rules in real time
and in daily batch. Monitoring is designed to detect structuring, rapid
movement of funds, and activity inconsistent with a customer's stated profile. Any
single cash-equivalent transaction at or above USD 10,000, or a series of linked
transactions that aggregate to that threshold within a rolling 24-hour period,
generates a mandatory alert for analyst review.

## 6. Suspicious Activity Reporting

When an analyst concludes that activity is suspicious, they must file a Suspicious
Activity Report (SAR) with the relevant Financial Intelligence Unit within 30
calendar days of detection. Employees must never inform a customer that a SAR has
been or may be filed; doing so ("tipping off") is prohibited and may constitute a
criminal offence. SAR decisions and their supporting rationale are documented and
retained by the Compliance team.

## 7. Sanctions Screening

All customers and counterparties are screened against applicable sanctions lists at
onboarding, on every list update, and before processing cross-border payments. A
confirmed sanctions match results in an immediate transaction block and escalation
to the Sanctions Officer. Northwind Pay does not process any transaction involving
a sanctioned party, jurisdiction, or vessel.

## 8. Record Keeping

Customer due-diligence records, transaction records, and SAR documentation are
retained for a minimum of five years after the end of the customer relationship or
the date of the transaction, whichever is later. Records must be retrievable within
a reasonable period upon a lawful request from a competent authority.

## 9. Training

All employees complete AML training at onboarding and at least annually thereafter.
Staff in customer-facing and transaction-monitoring roles receive additional
role-specific training. Completion is tracked, and failure to complete mandatory
training may result in revoked system access.

## 10. Roles and Responsibilities

The Board holds ultimate accountability for AML compliance. The Chief Compliance
Officer owns the program and reports to the Board quarterly. The Money Laundering
Reporting Officer (MLRO) is responsible for SAR decisions. All employees are
responsible for escalating suspicious activity through the designated channel.
