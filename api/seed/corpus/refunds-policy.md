# Customer Refunds and Chargebacks Policy

**Northwind Pay, Inc. — Synthetic sample document for demonstration purposes only.**
Policy owner: Head of Customer Operations. Version 1.4. Effective 1 April 2026.

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines when and how Northwind Pay issues refunds to customers and how it
handles card chargebacks and disputes. It balances fair customer outcomes against the
firm's obligation to detect and prevent refund-related fraud.

## 2. Refund Eligibility

A customer is eligible for a refund when a transaction was unauthorized, duplicated,
or charged in error, or when goods or services were not delivered as described.
Refund requests must be made within 90 days of the original transaction date. Requests
outside this window are reviewed case by case and require manager approval.

## 3. Refund Timeframes

Approved refunds are initiated within 2 business days of approval. Funds typically
settle to the customer's original payment method within 5 to 10 business days,
depending on the card network and issuing bank. Refunds are always returned to the
original payment method; cash or alternative-method refunds are not permitted.

## 4. Approval Thresholds

Refunds up to USD 500 may be approved by a front-line agent. Refunds from USD 500.01
to USD 5,000 require team-lead approval. Refunds above USD 5,000, and any refund on an
account with an open fraud investigation, require manager approval and a documented
justification. No employee may approve a refund to an account they own or control.

## 5. Fraud Controls

Refund activity is monitored for abuse, including refunds to a payment method
different from the original, repeated refunds to the same beneficiary, and refund
amounts that exceed the original charge. Any refund that would exceed the original
transaction amount is automatically blocked. Suspected refund fraud is escalated to
the Compliance team and may trigger the Suspicious Activity Reporting process.

## 6. Chargebacks and Disputes

When a customer disputes a transaction with their card issuer, Northwind Pay receives
a chargeback. The Disputes team gathers evidence — order records, delivery
confirmation, and customer communications — and responds within the card network's
deadline, which is typically no more than 7 calendar days from notification. Failure
to respond within the deadline forfeits the dispute.

## 7. Customer Communication

Customers are notified in writing when a refund is approved, declined, or requires
additional information. Declines must state a clear reason. All refund and dispute
decisions, along with their supporting rationale, are recorded in the case management
system and retained for at least five years.
